Welcome

A Tradition of Excellence in Nursing Education

Bhanja College of Nursing, nestled in the historic town of Bhanjanagar in the Ganjam district of Odisha, was established with a singular mission — to create skilled, empathetic, and professionally competent nurses who can serve communities across India and beyond.

Named in honour of the rich cultural heritage of the Bhanja dynasty that once flourished in this region, our institution brings together modern medical education and the timeless values of care and compassion. Since our founding, we have been committed to providing affordable, high-quality nursing education to students from rural and semi-urban backgrounds of southern Odisha.

Our college is approved by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, recognized by the Odisha Nurses and Midwives Council (ONMC), and affiliated to the Odisha University of Health Sciences (OUHS), Bhubaneswar. We offer three comprehensive programs — ANM, GNM, and B.Sc. Nursing — each designed to meet national healthcare standards and prepare graduates for rewarding careers in hospitals, community health centres, and public health organizations.

ANM

2 Years

The Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery program trains students in primary healthcare delivery, maternal and child health, and community nursing. Ideal for those seeking to serve at the grassroots level of India's healthcare system.
