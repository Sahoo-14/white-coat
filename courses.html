B.Sc. Nursing (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)

Duration: 4 Years Intake: 60 Seats Fee: ₹1,20,000/year (approx.)

Course Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.Sc. Nursing) is a 4-year undergraduate degree program that provides a strong foundation in biological, behavioral, and nursing sciences. This program prepares students for professional nursing practice, leadership roles, and advanced education. Graduates are eligible for registration as Registered Nurses and Registered Midwives (RN & RM) with the State Nursing Council.

The curriculum integrates classroom instruction with extensive clinical practice across various healthcare settings including hospitals, community health centres, and primary health centres. Students gain hands-on experience in medical-surgical nursing, pediatric nursing, obstetric nursing, psychiatric nursing, and community health nursing.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 examination passed with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology from a recognized board
  • Minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/OBC candidates)
  • Minimum age: 17 years at the time of admission
  • Medically fit as certified by a registered medical practitioner
  • Valid Odisha state domicile or as per admission norms

Year-wise Syllabus

  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Nutrition & Biochemistry
  • Nursing Foundations
  • Psychology
  • Microbiology
  • English
  • Computer Education
  • Hindi/Regional Language
  • Sociology
  • Pharmacology, Pathology & Genetics
  • Community Health Nursing - I
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing - I
  • Communication & Educational Technology
  • Environmental Science
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing - II
  • Child Health Nursing
  • Mental Health Nursing
  • Nursing Research & Statistics
  • Midwifery & Obstetrical Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing - II
  • Nursing Management & Leadership
  • Nursing Education

GNM (General Nursing & Midwifery)

Duration: 3 Years + 6 Months Internship Intake: 40 Seats Fee: ₹80,000/year (approx.)

Course Overview

The General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) program is a 3.5-year diploma course (3 years of study + 6 months of internship) that trains students to become competent general nurses and midwives. This program is ideal for students who wish to enter the nursing profession quickly with a comprehensive foundation in patient care, midwifery, and community health.

GNM graduates are eligible for registration as Registered Nurses and Registered Midwives with the Odisha Nurses & Midwives Council and can practice in government and private hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, and community health settings across India.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 examination passed with Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Minimum 40% aggregate marks from a recognized board
  • Minimum age: 17 years, Maximum age: 35 years
  • Medically fit

Year-wise Syllabus

  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Microbiology
  • Psychology & Sociology
  • Fundamentals of Nursing
  • First Aid
  • Personal Hygiene
  • Community Health Nursing - I
  • Health Education
  • Nutrition
  • English
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing - I & II
  • Pharmacology
  • Paediatric Nursing
  • Mental Health Nursing
  • Communication Skills
  • Midwifery & Gynaecological Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing - II
  • Nursing Administration & Ward Management
  • Professional Trends & Adjustments
  • Nursing Research

ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery)

Duration: 2 Years Intake: 30 Seats Fee: ₹60,000/year (approx.)

Course Overview

The Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery (ANM) is a 2-year diploma program focused on training healthcare workers for primary and community healthcare delivery. ANM professionals play a crucial role in India's public health system, working at Primary Health Centres (PHCs), Community Health Centres (CHCs), sub-centres, and villages.

This course covers maternal and child health, community health nursing, health promotion, disease prevention, and basic curative care. It is ideal for students passionate about serving rural communities and making a difference at the grassroots level of healthcare.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 pass from any recognized board (preferably with Science stream)
  • Minimum age: 17 years
  • Medically fit as certified by a registered medical practitioner

Year-wise Syllabus

  • Community Health Nursing
  • Health Promotion & Nutrition
  • Child Health Nursing
  • Environmental Sanitation
  • First Aid & Referral
  • Anatomy & Physiology (Basics)
  • Midwifery
  • Health Centre Management
  • Primary Healthcare & Disease Prevention
  • Family Welfare & National Health Programs
  • Practical Training & Field Visits
