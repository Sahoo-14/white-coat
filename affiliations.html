Our Regulatory Approvals

Bhanja College of Nursing meets all regulatory standards set by national and state-level nursing education authorities.

Indian Nursing Council (INC)

The Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi, is the apex regulatory body for nursing education in India, established under the Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947. INC prescribes the syllabus, regulations, and minimum standards for nursing programs across the country. Bhanja College of Nursing is duly approved by INC for conducting ANM, GNM, and B.Sc. Nursing programs.

INC approval ensures that our curriculum, infrastructure, faculty qualifications, clinical facilities, and student-teacher ratios meet nationally mandated standards for quality nursing education.

Visit INC Official Website

Odisha Nurses & Midwives Council (ONMC)

The Odisha Nurses and Midwives Council is the state-level registration body that recognizes nursing institutions and registers nursing graduates in Odisha. Recognition by ONMC is mandatory for nursing colleges operating in the state and for graduates seeking to practice as registered nurses within Odisha.

Our college's recognition by ONMC validates that we maintain all state-prescribed standards for nursing education delivery, clinical training partnerships, and examination protocols. Graduates of our programs are eligible for registration as Registered Nurses (RN) and Registered Midwives (RM) with the council.

Visit ONMC Website

Odisha University of Health Sciences (OUHS)

The Odisha University of Health Sciences, Bhubaneswar, is the affiliating university for health science education in Odisha. Established by the Government of Odisha, OUHS oversees academic standards, conducts examinations, and awards degrees and diplomas for all health science programs including nursing.

Bhanja College of Nursing is affiliated to OUHS for B.Sc. Nursing degree program. Our B.Sc. Nursing graduates receive their degree from OUHS, which is recognized across India and internationally. The university also monitors our academic calendar, examination schedules, and faculty development programs.

Visit OUHS Website
