Our History

A Legacy Rooted in Service

Bhanja College of Nursing was founded in the culturally rich town of Bhanjanagar, located in the Ganjam district of southern Odisha. The name "Bhanja" pays tribute to the illustrious Bhanja dynasty, whose rulers were renowned patrons of art, literature, and public welfare in this region for centuries. Inspired by this legacy of service to the community, the college was established to address the acute shortage of trained nursing professionals in rural and semi-urban areas of Odisha.

Bhanjanagar, the headquarters of the Bhanjanagar subdivision, serves as a vital link between the tribal hinterlands of Ganjam and the coastal plains. The town's strategic location made it an ideal site for a nursing institution that could draw students from across southern Odisha — including districts like Ganjam, Gajapati, Kandhamal, and Rayagada — and train them to serve their own communities.

Since its establishment, Bhanja College of Nursing has grown from a modest institution offering a single diploma course to a comprehensive nursing education centre with multiple programs approved by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi. The college received its first INC approval and subsequently added the GNM and B.Sc. Nursing programs, each time expanding its infrastructure, faculty, and clinical training partnerships.

Today, the college stands as one of the leading nursing institutions in the Ganjam district, with a sprawling campus featuring modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a dedicated library, hostel facilities, and strong affiliations with government and private hospitals for clinical rotations. Our alumni serve in prestigious hospitals across India and abroad, carrying forward the Bhanja legacy of compassion and excellence.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

Key moments that have shaped Bhanja College of Nursing

2015

Foundation & Establishment

Bhanja College of Nursing was founded in Bhanjanagar with the vision of providing quality nursing education to students of southern Odisha. The institution began with the ANM program and a modest batch of students.

2016

INC Approval for ANM Program

Received official approval from the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, for the Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery (ANM) program, marking a significant milestone in the institution's journey.

2017

First Batch Graduation

The first batch of ANM students graduated with distinction, many securing positions in government primary health centres across Ganjam and neighbouring districts.

2018

GNM Program Launched

The General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) diploma program was introduced following INC approval, expanding the college's offerings and attracting a larger student body from across Odisha.

2020

OUHS Affiliation & B.Sc. Nursing

Affiliated to Odisha University of Health Sciences (OUHS), Bhubaneswar, and launched the B.Sc. Nursing (4-year) degree program, elevating the institution to university-level education.

2022

Clinical Partnerships Expanded

Signed MoUs with multiple government and private hospitals in Ganjam district for enhanced clinical postings, providing students with diverse and rigorous practical training opportunities.

2024

Infrastructure Upgrades

Inaugurated the new simulation laboratory wing, upgraded the library with digital resources, and expanded hostel capacity, reinforcing the college's commitment to providing world-class facilities.

2026

A Decade of Excellence

Celebrating over a decade of nurturing compassionate healthcare professionals with 500+ alumni serving across India, the college continues its mission with renewed vigour and expanded programs.
